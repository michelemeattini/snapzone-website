Short version: SnapZone is a local-first app. It collects no personal data. This website stores only your theme and language preference — no tracking, no cookies, no analytics.

  • Name: Michele Meattini

For any privacy-related requests, including the exercise of your GDPR rights, you may contact the data controller directly at the address above.

2. What Data We Collect

2.1 — This Website

The SnapZone website (snapzone-seven.vercel.app) does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts. It does not collect any personally identifiable information.

The following data is stored locally in your browser (via localStorage) and never transmitted to any server:

KeyPurposeExpires
sz-themeRemembers your light/dark mode preferenceUntil cleared by user
sz-langRemembers your manually selected languageUntil cleared by user
sz-announce-dismissedHides the announcement banner after dismissalSession only

These values never leave your device and contain no personal information. They are strictly necessary for the website to function as expected.

2.2 — The SnapZone Application

The SnapZone macOS application is local-first. All processing happens on your device. The application does not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Window titles or application names
  • Trackpad gesture recordings or raw input data
  • Usage statistics or behavioral analytics
  • Crash reports or diagnostics
  • Any personal or device-identifying information

Usage statistics displayed within the app (e.g. total snaps, time saved) are calculated and stored exclusively on your local device and are never transmitted externally.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 — Hosting (Vercel)

This website is hosted on Vercel Inc. (San Francisco, CA, USA). Vercel may collect standard web server logs (IP address, browser user agent, request path, timestamp) as part of normal server operation. These logs are retained for a short period for security and operational purposes. Vercel's privacy policy is available at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

3.2 — Payment Processor (Lemon Squeezy)

Purchases of the SnapZone Lifetime License are processed by Lemon Squeezy (a service of Lemon Squeezy, LLC). When you make a purchase, you will be redirected to Lemon Squeezy's checkout at snapzone.lemonsqueezy.com. Lemon Squeezy acts as the Merchant of Record and collects the billing information necessary to process the transaction (name, email, payment details). SnapZone does not receive or store your payment card data. Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy is available at lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.

3.3 — Software Updates (Sparkle)

The application uses the Sparkle open-source framework to deliver automatic updates. When the app checks for updates, it contacts our update server to download the appcast file (appcast.xml). This request may include your IP address as part of the standard HTTP request, but no additional personal data is transmitted. No usage data or analytics are collected as part of this process.

3.4 — Embedded Video (YouTube)

The homepage includes a demo video embedded from YouTube using the privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). According to Google's documentation, this domain does not store cookies on your device unless you interact with the video player. If you play the video, YouTube may collect data as described in the Google Privacy Policy. The video is loaded with loading="lazy" and will not connect to YouTube servers until the page section is scrolled into view.

3.5 — Fonts

All fonts used on this website are self-hosted. No requests are made to Google Fonts, Adobe Fonts, or any external font service. Font files are served directly from our own servers.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the legal bases for any processing described in this policy are:

  • No processing: All other interactions with this website involve no personal data processing.

Any transfers of personal data to these processors are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as approved by the European Commission, or by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where applicable.

  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) — Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17) — Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restriction (Art. 18) — Request that we limit the processing of your data.
  • Right to portability (Art. 20) — Request your data in a machine-readable format.
